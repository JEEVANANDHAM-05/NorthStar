FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about tax filing in India.

Any individual with income above the basic exemption limit (₹2.5 lakh for those below 60 years) must file an ITR. It's also mandatory if you have foreign income, own foreign assets, or wish to claim a refund.
You'll need your PAN card, Aadhaar, Form 16 (from employer), bank account details, investment proof (80C, 80D), and details of any other income sources like rent or capital gains.
For most salaried individuals, we complete the filing within 24 hours of receiving all documents. Complex cases such as business returns may take 2–3 working days.
After your ITR is filed and verified (e-verification via Aadhaar OTP), the Income Tax Department typically processes refunds within 2–6 weeks, directly into your bank account.
You can securely share your documents directly with our tax experts via WhatsApp or email. We do not host, store, or receive your documents on our servers, ensuring your sensitive financial data remains completely confidential.
For most individuals, the ITR filing deadline is July 31st of the assessment year (e.g., July 31, 2025 for AY 2025-26). Business taxpayers requiring audit have until October 31st. Late filing attracts a penalty up to ₹5,000.
